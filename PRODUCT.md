# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Developers, solopreneurs, technical leads, content creators and researchers who want to build or operate modular, multimodal autonomous agents in Claude Code, Codex and Hermes. They pay for tokens and demand absolute cost control, predictability and audited code with no black boxes. Situation: a terminal open beside the browser, evaluating whether a harness deserves their account and their credits; most sessions in the evening.

## Product Purpose

OmniHarness is a complete agent-engineering ecosystem that orchestrates the whole lifecycle of an agentic demand: capability mapping as a knowledge graph (skills-graph) with on-demand activation and installation, active research of technical and market references (scout), controlled divergent thinking (detour), mandatory human alignment (grilling, the plan gate), formal specification (to-spec), top-tier design (impeccable) and audited implementation with visual TDD (ponytail). Success for a user: a demand becomes a plan of skills, every spend and every network call waited for their yes, and the result is measured by benchmarks that can fail. The community portal is the product's entry point: it must make the mechanism intelligible in seconds, present what is open and what members get, and prove itself with its own measurements.

## Positioning

Context and Graph Spec-Driven Development: the plan is derived from a scout's dossier and a graph of the skills the hosts can actually reach, the human is a hard gate on direction and on spending, and nothing ships without a benchmark that could have failed. Neighbouring tools offer prompts, skill packs or agent frameworks; none makes the human gate, the graph and the measured proof one mechanism, nor keeps a living, comprehensive curation: not only tools and MCP servers but a permanently updated catalog of complete workflows, autonomous pipelines, hooks, operating routines and specialised agents ready to run in different experimental scenarios.

## Operating Context

Three hosts read one repository of portable skills (Agent Skills standard, six frontmatter fields). Claude Code additionally runs the Workflow drivers (Gauntlet, scout) and the hook-enforced gate. Workflows: `/omniharness` at session start; `/scout` demand → dossier → plan gate → PLAN.md; `/skill-installer` for tools; `/gauntlet-loop` on demand or on a benchmark regression; `thesis-review` for manuscripts. Documents of record: `AGENTS.md` (rules), `CONTEXT.md` (terms), `docs/adr/` (decisions), `docs/benchmarks.md` (numbers), `docs/scout/<slug>/` (plans). Evaluation: `python -m unittest discover tests`, the Node stub tests, `evals/run.py` paid arms behind the gate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Business model, open-core: a free core (open source, MIT: the base harness, the essential scripts and the open skill specifications); a premium members layer (the vault of practical assets: rigorously tested prompts, ready workflows, audit batteries with Gauntlet loops, specialised designs for proven real cases, end-to-end tutorials; an integrated consulting agent is on the roadmap, not shipped); specialised consulting and enterprise services (architecture, custom setup, implementation for companies and solopreneurs). Prices: undecided, never to be invented. Members' door: a private claude.ai edition of the portal shared by the maintainer; no credential form, no backend. Constraints: no paid call or network access without an explicit gate; parity between hosts; deterministic zero-token tests wherever possible; one static page, no build step, libraries only from cdnjs or jsDelivr, fonts from Google Fonts; GitHub Pages for the public edition. Terminology: invariant, gate, plan gate, ring, edge, dossier, routine, detour, arm, checkpoint, jaVistos, parecer (see CONTEXT.md).

## Brand Commitments

Name: OmniHarness. Voice: measured facts, the product's own words, no hype; every number carries its command and its date. Binding: radical transparency (no fake forms, no invented metrics, no simulated backend); the five invariants stay in the footer; calibration for finish: Linear, the Vercel changelog, Raycast (the owner's named bar, 2026-09-11).

## Evidence on Hand

`docs/benchmarks.md` (B1–B5, measured on the reference machine, dated); `site/showcase/` (the record of the v2 build, log with timestamps, metrics, the scout run, the installer plan, the detour answer); `site/showcase/v3/shots/` (Playwright captures of v1 and v2 with `report.json` provenance); `docs/scout/portal-v2/dossier.md` (26 references); `docs/experiments/2026-09-10-graphify-skills-corpus.md`. Absent, never to be fabricated: users, testimonials, customers, prices, adoption numbers, Codex or Hermes end-to-end runs (documented as untested on the reference machine).

## Product Principles

1. The human is a gate, not an audience: spending, network and direction wait for a yes.
2. Nothing is claimed that a benchmark could not have failed.
3. The plan comes from evidence: a dossier, a graph, a spec.
4. Open by default, valuable in the vault: the core is MIT, the members' assets are the product.
5. One source of truth per fact, and the page reads from it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced-motion preference honoured (no physics or reveals in motion); light and dark themes each composed; body text contrast at least 4.5:1; keyboard focus visible; the graph's information also available as text (drawer, library rows).
