## Direction contract (v3: replacement world)

THESIS: the page is a collider event display, and one session is one event. The skills graph is the event: tracks curling out of the collision into three detector rings (installed, catalog, remote), the human gate is the trigger that decides which events are recorded, and the benchmarks are deposited energy read off calorimeter bars. It refuses the hero-plus-cards docs template and the dark neon tool page: no gradient, no glow, colour only on tracks and bars.

OWN-WORLD: near-black vacuum ground (`#0C0E13` since v3.1, `#0A0E15` in v3; never pure black), detector rings and rules in muted steel blue, tracks in signal colours by type (cyan calls, yellow precedes, green feeds, violet guided-by, dashed for alternative-to and candidate-for), red bars for spend, an indigo accent for interaction only (v3.1), technical grotesk labels pinned to tracks (Geist for display and reading, Geist Mono for figures and labels since v3.1; Bricolage Grotesque, Schibsted Grotesk and Azeret Mono in v3), tabular figures everywhere a number sits, label plates that name every zone literally (Event, Trigger, Layers, Run log, Shift crew, Calorimeter, Events 1–3, Contribute). Light theme: the publication figure, white ground, ink rings, the same coloured tracks at print-safe values. Recognisable with all content removed: the rings, the beamline index on the left, the plates.
